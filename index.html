When Heaven Drew Near

Silence covered Nazareth. Ordinary routines unfolded beneath ordinary skies. A young woman prayed, unaware that history itself was preparing to bend toward her life. Overshadowed: God With Us follows Mary's journey through fear, surrender, faith, and divine purpose as God revealed Himself through Jesus. Walk through the silence before the voice, the weight of surrender, and the wonder of God dwelling among humanity.
