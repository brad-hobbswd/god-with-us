Final Prayer

Lord, Overshadow My Life

Mary's story reminds every heart that heaven still draws near. The same God who moved through silence and surrender continues working through willing lives today.

The presence of God still meets surrendered hearts.

Jesus, Thank You for drawing near. Thank You for stepping into humanity and revealing Yourself through love, mercy, and purpose. Teach me to trust You during silent seasons. Give me courage when fear rises and strengthen my heart when questions remain unanswered. Prepare my life for Your purpose. Let surrender become stronger than uncertainty. Let faith become greater than fear. Overshadow my thoughts, my decisions, my family, and my future with Your presence. Let my heart remain willing. Let my life remain available. Let Your will become greater than my own. In the name of Jesus, Amen.

The story continues. God with us. Still near. Still faithful. Still moving.
