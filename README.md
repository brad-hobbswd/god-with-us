# Overshadowed: God With Us

A cinematic faith journey through silence, surrender, divine presence, and the revelation of Jesus.

This book follows Mary through ordinary moments transformed by extraordinary purpose. Nazareth, Bethlehem, fear, favor, obedience, and fulfillment unfold through a story centered on one truth:

**God came near.**

Created and written by **Bradley Hobbs**

## About the Book

*Overshadowed: God With Us* follows the movement of heaven entering ordinary life.

Readers journey through:

• The silence before God's voice  
• Fear meeting favor  
• The weight of surrender  
• The overshadowing presence of God  
• Bethlehem and incarnation  
• Obedience through misunderstanding  
• Fulfillment of divine promise  

The story reveals a simple yet powerful truth:

God did not remain distant.

God with us.
